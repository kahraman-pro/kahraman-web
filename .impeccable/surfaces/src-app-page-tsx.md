Scope: every route (home, services, service detail, work, case study, blog, post, about, contact, privacy, app legal pages, 404). Mode: Persuade (home, services, work); Read (blog, legal, privacy).
Audience: founders and managers of small and medium teams, Upwork clients checking the profile, occasionally employers. Action: send a short message or book a call. Constraints: copy verbatim, layout and section order free (user, 2026-11-26); MK monogram kept, colour may change; Tailwind utilities only; avoid the agency/corporate template.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a letter from one person: letterhead, direct address, plain figures, a signed close. Refuses the agency template (floating glass bar, card rows under soft shadows) and the previous ledger-blue/bordo Onest look.

OWN-WORLD: Envelope grey-blue ground #eceff3 around one long bond-white sheet #fcfcfb. Fountain-pen ink #1c2f6e for type, logo and the one filled button; postmark red #c23a2b only for the stamp, focus and tiny markers; blotting-blue wash #eef2f8 for wells and hover. Young Serif for headings, figures and the name; Schibsted Grotesk for everything else. Hairline ink rules, 10px controls, 6px sheet corners, no card shadows on the sheet.

STORY: The visitor reads a letterhead, the offer in one line, the action and four figures; then enclosures (case work), services as a ruled list, the person, principles, numbers, the process as numbered points, and a signed close with the email.
